Private limited company registration

Incorporation is a single integrated form now. Getting it right the first time matters, because correcting a wrong structure later costs more than setting it up properly did.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

The structure comes before the paperwork. A private limited company suits a business that will raise funds, add shareholders or hold a licence; an LLP suits a professional or family venture that wants limited liability without a board; a proprietorship suits a business that is genuinely one person. We say which one fits before quoting for any of them.

Name approval is the most common stopping point. Names are refused for resembling an existing company, conflicting with a registered trademark, or using a word that requires approval. Checking the trademark register alongside the company register at the start saves a rejected application.

What we handle

  • Structure advice — private limited, LLP, OPC, partnership or proprietorship
  • Name search against the company register and the trademark register, and reservation
  • Digital signature certificates and director identification numbers
  • MOA and AOA drafted for your actual business objects, not a generic template
  • Incorporation through SPICe+ with AGILE-PRO, including PAN, TAN, EPFO and ESIC
  • Registered office documentation, including the owner's NOC and utility proof
  • Bank account opening documents and the board resolution the bank will ask for
  • Commencement of business filing in Form INC-20A after capital is brought in
  • Shareholders' agreement and founder arrangements, drafted on the legal side of the firm
  • Post-incorporation register maintenance, first board meeting and statutory records

What you supply

  • PAN and Aadhaar of every proposed director and subscriber
  • Passport-size photograph and email and mobile of each director
  • Address proof of each director — bank statement or utility bill, recent
  • Passport for any foreign national or non-resident director
  • Registered office proof — utility bill, and rent agreement or ownership document
  • No objection certificate from the owner of the registered office premises
  • Two or three proposed names, in order of preference

Name preferences are worth thinking about before you send them. If the business will eventually need a trademark, choosing a name that can actually be registered saves a rebrand later.

Read this before filing

The registered office is not a formality

Every notice from the department, the registry and the tax authority goes to the registered office address. If it is a relative's premises or a temporary space, decide now how post will reach you — a missed notice at that address is treated as a delivered one.

Questions we are asked about this

How many people do I need to start a private limited company?

Two shareholders and two directors, and one of the directors must be resident in India. A one person company is available where there is only one promoter, with its own restrictions.

How long does incorporation take?

Once name approval is in hand and all documents are signed, it is usually a matter of days rather than weeks. Document readiness — particularly the registered office NOC and address proofs — is the real variable.

What does it cost?

Two components: our professional fee, and the government fee, which varies with the state of registration, the authorised capital and stamp duty. We quote both separately in writing before starting. Anyone giving a single all-India number without asking about your state is guessing.

What compliance follows after incorporation?

Commencement of business within the prescribed period, an auditor appointed, annual financial statements and annual return with the registry, director KYC each year, and income tax returns — whether or not the company has traded. We set out the calendar at the time of incorporation.

Tell us what the business will actually do

The activity, the number of founders and whether outside funding is likely will settle the structure in one conversation.
