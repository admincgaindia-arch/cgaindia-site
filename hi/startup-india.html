Aksar poochhe jaane wale sawal

Kya CGA grant ki guarantee deta hai?

Nahi. Sanction sirf ministry, state department ya incubator karta hai. Hum eligibility, documentation aur application ko itna strong banate hain ki reject hone ke common reasons na rahein.

Sabse pehle kya karna chahiye?

Private Limited ya LLP ke roop mein incorporation aur phir DPIIT recognition. Zyadatar schemes isi ke baad khulti hain.

Grant aur loan mein kya fark hai?

Grant wapas nahi karna hota. Loan ya debt wapas karna hota hai, aur convertible ya equity-linked support mein founders ki shareholding dilute hoti hai.

Kya ek saath do schemes le sakte hain?

Same purpose ke liye aam taur par nahi. Alag purpose aur stage ke liye kai schemes combine ho sakti hain; hum pehle conflict check karte hain.

Proprietorship ko funding mil sakti hai?

Startup India aur zyadatar grant schemes ke liye nahi. MSME schemes jaise PMEGP aur CGTMSE loans ke liye haan.

CGA eligibility review, documentation, filing aur compliance support deta hai. Recognition, grant, loan ya certificate ka sanction sirf implementing authority karti hai; CGA approval ya amount ki guarantee nahi deta. Scheme limits latest official guidelines ke hisaab se badal sakti hain.

Kaunsi scheme fit hai, pata nahi?

Company ka type, incorporation date, state aur product bata dijiye. Hum 15 minute mein bata denge. Iske liye koi charge nahi.
