Foreign income and NRI taxation

Residential status decides everything else. Get it right first, and the rest of an NRI return becomes ordinary work.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

Your status is a question of days present in India, tested against thresholds that changed in recent years and that treat Indian-source income differently. Two people with identical jobs abroad can end up with different statuses, and therefore with a different answer on whether foreign salary is taxable in India at all.

The second half of the work is disclosure. Foreign bank accounts, shares, retirement accounts and property generally have to be reported by a resident even where no income arises from them, and the consequences of leaving them out are disproportionate to the tax involved.

What we handle

  • Residential status determination, including resident but not ordinarily resident cases
  • Taxability of foreign salary, and relief where the same income is taxed in both countries
  • Treaty relief under a DTAA, with Form 67 and the tax residency certificate
  • Schedule FA reporting — foreign bank accounts, shares, retirement funds and immovable property
  • NRO and NRE account interest, and TDS deducted by Indian banks on NRO income
  • Capital gains on Indian shares, mutual funds and property held by a non-resident
  • Sale of property in India by an NRI, including the buyer's withholding obligation
  • Lower deduction certificates under section 197 where TDS exceeds the actual liability
  • Repatriation of funds with Form 15CA and Form 15CB certification
  • Returning Indians, and the year in which status changes

What you supply

  • Passport pages showing arrival and departure dates for the relevant years
  • Overseas employment contract and salary statements
  • Tax return and tax paid record in the other country, if claiming treaty relief
  • Tax residency certificate from the other country's authority, where required
  • Indian bank statements, including NRO and NRE accounts
  • Details of foreign accounts, shares, pension funds and property held
  • Sale documents and purchase records, if property is being sold

Day-count is the foundation of the whole return. Send travel dates before anything else, even if the rest of the documents take time.

Before the sale, not after

Selling property in India as an NRI?

The buyer must withhold tax on the sale, and the rate applied to the sale value is often far higher than the tax actually due on your gain. A certificate under section 197 obtained before the transaction avoids locking up funds for a year waiting for a refund.

Questions we are asked about this

I am an NRI. Do I have to file a return in India at all?

If you have income arising in India above the basic limit — rent, interest, capital gains, professional fees — then generally yes. Many NRIs also file to claim a refund of TDS deducted by banks at rates well above their actual liability.

Is my salary earned abroad taxable in India?

For a non-resident, foreign salary for services rendered outside India generally is not. For a resident, worldwide income is taxable, with credit available for tax paid abroad under the relevant treaty. Which of the two you are is the whole question.

What is the risk of not reporting a foreign account?

Non-disclosure of foreign assets by a resident is treated far more seriously than an ordinary omission, with its own penalty regime independent of the tax involved. The disclosure obligation applies even where the account earns nothing.

Can you handle everything without me coming to India?

Yes. Documents are collected and returned digitally, signatures are managed through e-verification or a power of attorney where the authority requires one, and we deal with the bank directly on repatriation.
