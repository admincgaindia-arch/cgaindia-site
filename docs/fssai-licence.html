FSSAI licence and registration

Three tiers, and applying under the wrong one is the most common reason a food business ends up re-applying.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

Which licence you need is decided by turnover, the nature of the operation, and whether you operate in more than one state. A manufacturer, an importer and a home kitchen sit at different tiers, and an operation that crosses a threshold mid-year has to upgrade rather than wait for renewal.

Food businesses also carry obligations after the licence is granted — display of the licence number, annual return for certain categories, and a hygiene standard that is inspected rather than declared. We set those out at the start instead of leaving them to the first inspection.

What we handle

  • Tier assessment — basic registration, state licence or central licence
  • Application with product category selection matched to what you actually make or sell
  • Licences for manufacturers, restaurants, cloud kitchens, traders, transporters and storage
  • Importer licence, which is central regardless of turnover
  • E-commerce food seller registration for marketplace listings
  • Renewal before expiry, and modification for added products or premises
  • Annual return filing where the category requires it
  • Layout and equipment documentation for manufacturing licences
  • Reply to improvement notices and inspection observations
  • Label review against packaging and display requirements

What you supply

  • Identity and address proof of the proprietor, partners or directors
  • Premises proof — rent agreement or ownership document, with a utility bill
  • List of food products or categories handled
  • Layout plan of the premises, for a manufacturing licence
  • List of equipment and machinery, with capacity, for manufacturers
  • Water test report from a recognised laboratory, where required
  • Form IX nomination, and the food safety management plan

Turnover and capacity decide the tier, so send realistic figures rather than conservative ones. Applying at a lower tier and crossing the threshold later means applying again.

Applies to more businesses than expected

Selling food online or from home?

Cloud kitchens, home bakers and marketplace food sellers are food businesses under the Act. Most marketplaces will not accept a listing without the number, and the basic registration tier exists precisely for operations of this size.

Questions we are asked about this

I run a small tea stall. Do I need this?

A food business at the smallest scale generally needs basic registration rather than a licence. It is inexpensive and it is the tier designed for exactly that operation.

How long is a licence valid?

It can be taken for a period of one to five years, and renewal has to be applied for before expiry. Allowing it to lapse means applying afresh, with the business unlicensed in the meantime.

We are adding a new product line. Does the existing licence cover it?

Only if the category was declared. Adding products outside the declared categories requires a modification of the licence, which is a simpler process than a fresh application but is not optional.

Do I need a separate licence for each outlet?

Generally each premises needs its own registration or licence, and operations across states can push you to the central tier. The count of outlets and their locations settles this.

Tell us what you make or sell, and where

Product, turnover and number of states — three facts that settle the tier before any application is made.
