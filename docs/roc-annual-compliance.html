ROC annual compliance

Annual filings carry a daily additional fee once the date passes, and it does not stop accruing. This is the one compliance head where delay has a running price tag.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

Registry compliance is unforgiving in a specific way: the additional fee for late filing runs per day, without a ceiling in most cases, and directors of a company that fails to file for a continued period face disqualification. A dormant company that never traded is not exempt from any of this.

The work is also more than two forms. Board meetings have to be held and minuted, an auditor appointed and any casual vacancy filled, registers maintained, and event-based forms filed when a director changes, capital is allotted or a charge is created.

What we handle

  • Financial statements filed in Form AOC-4, with XBRL where applicable
  • Annual return in Form MGT-7 or MGT-7A for small companies and OPCs
  • LLP annual filings — Form 8 statement of account and Form 11 annual return
  • Director KYC each year in DIR-3 KYC or the web-based confirmation
  • Board meeting and annual general meeting notices, minutes and resolutions
  • Auditor appointment in Form ADT-1, and filling of a casual vacancy
  • Statutory registers — members, directors, charges and share transfers
  • Event-based filings — director change, registered office shift, share allotment and transfer
  • Charge creation, modification and satisfaction with the registry
  • Condonation and compounding where filings were missed in earlier years

What you supply

  • Trial balance and final accounts for the year
  • Audit report and the auditor's signed financial statements
  • Board and general meeting details for the year, with attendance
  • Details of any director change, allotment or charge during the year
  • Previous year's filed forms and their challans
  • Digital signatures of the director signing, and of the professional certifying

If earlier years are also pending, send those details at the same time. The forms are sequential and the cost of clearing a backlog is easier to quote once, not year by year.

Costs run daily

Missed last year's filing?

The additional fee accrues per day of delay, so the answer to “shall we do it next month” is always that next month is more expensive. Where the delay is long, condonation or compounding may be the cleaner route, and we will say which applies.

Questions we are asked about this

My company has no business activity. Do I still need to file?

Yes. Annual filings, director KYC and income tax returns apply to a dormant company as much as to a trading one. If the company is genuinely not going to trade, striking it off or obtaining dormant status is a decision worth taking rather than leaving it to accumulate fees.

What happens if filings are missed for years?

Additional fees accumulate, the company can be struck off by the registry, and directors can be disqualified for a period, which affects their directorships in other companies as well. The position is recoverable, but the route depends on how long it has run.

Is an LLP treated the same way?

The forms differ — Form 8 and Form 11 rather than AOC-4 and MGT-7 — and the due dates are different, but the daily additional fee for delay applies equally. LLPs are not the lighter option that they are often assumed to be on this point.

Can you take over from our existing consultant mid-year?

Yes, and it is common. We start by reconciling what is on the registry record against what we are told has been filed — those two do not always agree.
