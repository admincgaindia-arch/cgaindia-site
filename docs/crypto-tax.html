Crypto and VDA taxation

Gains on virtual digital assets are taxed under their own regime — flat rate, no set-off of losses, and a schedule in the return that has to match your exchange statements.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

The rules for virtual digital assets do not follow ordinary capital gains. Income is taxed at a flat rate with only the cost of acquisition allowed as a deduction, loss from one asset cannot be set off against gain from another, and nothing can be carried forward. A profitable year and a loss-making year can both produce tax.

The reporting side is where most people get caught. Exchanges deduct tax under section 194S and report it, so the department already has a picture of your trading before you file. If Schedule VDA does not reconcile with that, the return invites questions.

What we handle

  • Transaction-wise computation under section 115BBH from exchange and wallet statements
  • Cost of acquisition working, including transfers between wallets and exchanges
  • Schedule VDA preparation, matched to the transaction record
  • TDS credit under section 194S reconciled against Form 26AS and the AIS
  • Treatment of airdrops, staking rewards, mining receipts and gifts of digital assets
  • Peer-to-peer and foreign exchange transactions, including bank enquiry replies
  • Schedule FA reporting where assets are held on a foreign platform
  • Income from trading treated as business income, where the facts support it
  • Earlier years left unreported, through an updated return where available
  • Replies to notices arising from AIS entries for digital asset transactions

What you supply

  • Full-year transaction statements from every exchange used, in the exported format
  • Wallet addresses or statements for off-exchange holdings and transfers
  • Bank statements covering deposits to and withdrawals from exchanges
  • Details of any assets received as a gift, airdrop, staking reward or mining output
  • Form 26AS and the AIS download for the year

Send the raw exported files rather than a summary. Cost of acquisition has to be traced transaction by transaction, and a summary cannot be audited back to source.

Worth knowing

A loss year can still produce a tax liability

Because losses on one digital asset cannot be set off against gains on another, a portfolio that finished the year down can still carry tax on its winning trades. The computation has to be done trade-wise before you assume there is nothing to pay.

Questions we are asked about this

Can I set off crypto losses against my salary or share gains?

No. The regime for virtual digital assets does not permit set-off against other heads, and loss within the category cannot be carried forward either. This is the single biggest difference from ordinary capital gains.

The exchange already deducted TDS. Is my tax paid?

No. Deduction under 194S is a credit against your liability, not the liability itself. It is generally a small percentage of the transaction value, while the tax is charged on the gain at a flat rate.

I traded on a foreign exchange. Does that change anything?

It can add reporting obligations — holdings on a foreign platform may need to be disclosed in Schedule FA, and the remittance route matters. It is worth settling before filing rather than after.

I did not report earlier years. What now?

Depending on how far back it goes, an updated return may be available on payment of additional tax. Given that exchange data is reported, voluntary correction is a far better position than waiting.
