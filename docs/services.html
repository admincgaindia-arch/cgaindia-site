Choosing the structure, then getting it on the register — and the filings that follow in the first year.

  • One person companyIncorporation for a single promoter, with the nominee and the restrictions explained.
  • Partnership firm and proprietorshipDeed drafting, registration where required, PAN and the registrations that follow.
  • Section 8 company, trust and societyFormation for charitable objects, with the governing document drafted for the activity.
  • ROC annual complianceAOC-4, MGT-7, director KYC, minutes and statutory registers.

  • Overseas incorporationEntity set-up abroad, coordinated with Indian reporting under FEMA.

Tax and recurring compliance

CBDT · CBIC

The work that quietly decides whether you receive a notice — filed on a fixed cycle rather than at the deadline.

  • Income tax return filingIndividuals, firms and companies, reconciled against 26AS and the AIS.
  • TDS computation and returnsSection-wise computation, challan mapping, 24Q/26Q/27Q and Form 16.
  • GST registrationApplication, clarification replies and GSTIN, including e-commerce sellers.
  • GST return filingGSTR-1 and 3B with 2B reconciliation and input credit review.
  • GST annual return and auditGSTR-9 and 9C, with the year reconciled before it is certified.
  • GST refundsExport without payment of tax, inverted duty structure and excess cash balance.
  • Tax audit and statutory auditAudit under the Income-tax Act and the Companies Act, with the report and annexures.
  • Accounting and bookkeepingMonthly books, reconciliations and management reporting on a fixed calendar.
  • Payroll processingSalary computation, TDS on salary, PF and ESI returns and Form 16.
  • CMA data and project reportsEstimated and provisional statements prepared for bank credit proposals.
  • Notices, scrutiny and appealsGST and income tax notices, assessment representation and appeals.
  • Crypto and VDA taxationSection 115BBH computation, 194S credit and Schedule VDA.
