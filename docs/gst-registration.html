GST registration

The application is the easy part. Getting through physical verification and the clarification notice without losing three weeks is the work.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

Registration is granted on documents, not on intention. Most rejections and REG-03 clarification notices come down to three things: a principal place of business the officer cannot connect to the applicant, a rent agreement or electricity bill in a name that does not match, or a business activity that does not fit the HSN and constitution declared.

We check applicability before applying. Threshold, state, inter-state supply, e-commerce sale and reverse charge each change the answer, and a registration taken when none was required creates a permanent return obligation.

What we handle

  • Applicability review — turnover threshold, state, inter-state supply and compulsory registration cases
  • Regular, composition, casual taxable person and non-resident taxable person registration
  • Application in Form REG-01 with document preparation and Aadhaar authentication
  • Reply to clarification notices in Form REG-03 within the reply window
  • Physical verification support, including premises documentation
  • Additional place of business, and multi-state registration for sellers holding stock elsewhere
  • Amendment of core and non-core fields — address, partners, directors, business activity
  • Revocation of cancelled registration, and reply to cancellation notices in Form REG-17
  • Voluntary registration where a buyer insists on it, with the obligations explained first
  • Input service distributor and e-commerce operator registration where applicable

What you supply

  • PAN of the business, and of every partner, director or proprietor
  • Aadhaar of the authorised signatory, with the linked mobile number
  • Proof of principal place of business — ownership document, rent agreement, or a consent letter with the owner's document
  • A recent electricity bill, property tax receipt or municipal record for the premises
  • Constitution document — partnership deed, certificate of incorporation, or society or trust deed
  • Bank account proof — cancelled cheque or the first page of the statement
  • Photographs of the proprietor, partners or directors

Where the premises is taken from a family member without a written agreement, say so at the start. That situation has a standard fix, but not one that can be improvised after the notice arrives.

Time-bound

Registration suspended or cancelled?

A suspension under Form REG-17 carries a short reply window, and revocation gets harder once returns pile up behind it. Send the notice the day it arrives.

Questions we are asked about this

What is the turnover limit for registration?

It depends on the state and on whether you supply goods, services or both, and the special category states have their own limit. Several situations require registration regardless of turnover — inter-state supply of goods, e-commerce sale, and liability under reverse charge among them. We confirm your position before applying.

How long does registration take?

Where Aadhaar authentication succeeds and the documents are consistent, it is usually a matter of days. A clarification notice or physical verification adds to that, which is exactly why the document check happens before filing.

Can I register from home?

Yes, a residential address can be the principal place of business, supported by an ownership document or a consent letter from the owner along with proof of their ownership.

Do I need separate registration in each state?

Registration is state-wise. If you hold stock or have a place of business in another state — including inventory at a marketplace warehouse — that state needs its own registration.

Tell us what you sell and from where

Two facts — the nature of supply and the states involved — usually settle whether you need one registration or four.
