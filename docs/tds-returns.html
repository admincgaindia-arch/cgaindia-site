TDS computation and returns

Deduct at the right rate under the right section, deposit on time, and file a return that does not come back as a default six months later.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

Most TDS demands are not caused by non-payment. They are caused by a PAN typed wrong, a challan mapped to the wrong quarter, or a payment deducted under the wrong section — small clerical facts that TRACES turns into interest and a short-deduction notice.

We treat the return as the last step, not the first. The section, the rate, the threshold and the challan are settled when the payment is made, so the quarterly filing becomes an assembly job rather than a reconstruction.

What we handle

  • Section-wise applicability — 192, 194A, 194C, 194H, 194I, 194J, 194Q, 194-IA, 194-IB and 195
  • Rate and threshold checks, including higher rates for non-filers and missing PAN
  • Monthly deposit by challan, with the section and assessment year checked before payment
  • Quarterly returns in Form 24Q, 26Q and 27Q, and 27EQ for TCS
  • Form 16 and Form 16A generation and issue to deductees
  • Correction statements for PAN errors, challan mismatch and short deduction
  • Lower or nil deduction certificates under section 197
  • Section 194-IA on property purchase, including Form 26QB and the buyer's Form 16B
  • Reply to TRACES default notices, late fee under 234E and interest computation
  • Salary TDS working for employers, including regime declarations from employees

What you supply

  • TAN, and PAN of the entity
  • Vendor and employee PAN list with payment details for the quarter
  • Challans already paid, with BSR code, date and serial number
  • Salary structure and employee declarations, for 24Q
  • Previous quarter's return and any TRACES notice received

If you are unsure whether a payment attracted TDS at all, send the invoice — the section is easier to settle from the document than from a description.

Questions we are asked about this

We deducted the tax but deposited it late. How bad is that?

Interest runs under section 201(1A) from the date of deduction to the date of deposit, and the expenditure can be disallowed in some situations until the deposit is made. It is far better resolved now than at assessment.

Do we need to file a return for a quarter with no deductions?

There is no return to file if nothing was deducted, but filing a declaration of non-filing on TRACES for that quarter prevents the system from treating it as a pending return and issuing reminders.

A vendor says the credit is not showing in their 26AS.

That is almost always a PAN error or an unmapped challan in the return. It is fixed by a correction statement, and the credit then flows to the vendor for the same quarter.

I bought a flat. Is this the service I need?

Yes, in part. A buyer must deduct under section 194-IA and file Form 26QB, then issue Form 16B to the seller. We handle that, including cases where the deduction was missed and interest has to be worked out.
