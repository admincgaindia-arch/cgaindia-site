Trademark registration

The search matters more than the filing. Most refusals are visible on the public register before an application is ever made.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

A mark is registered class by class, for the goods or services actually covered. Filing in the wrong class leaves the business you actually run unprotected, and filing in every class costs money for protection you cannot defend.

An examination report is not a rejection. It is a stage most applications pass through, and it carries a reply window. Replied to properly, with evidence of use where the objection is about distinctiveness, a large share of these applications proceed.

What we handle

  • Search of the public register, and advice on whether the mark is worth filing as proposed
  • Class selection under the Nice classification for your actual goods and services
  • Application in Form TM-A, for a word mark, device mark or both
  • Reply to the examination report, with evidence of use where distinctiveness is questioned
  • Show cause hearings before the Registrar
  • Opposition filed against a conflicting mark, and defence of an opposition against yours
  • Renewal, assignment, and recording a change of proprietor name or address
  • Copyright registration for artistic work, literary work and software
  • Design registration for the shape and appearance of a product
  • International filing through the Madrid protocol, and national filings abroad

What you supply

  • The mark — the exact wording, or the logo file in a clear format
  • A list of the goods or services it is used for, in plain description
  • Date of first use, and evidence of it if the mark is already in the market
  • Applicant details — PAN, and constitution document for a company, LLP or firm
  • Udyam or MSME certificate, where a concessional fee is claimed
  • Power of attorney in Form TM-48, which we prepare for signature

If the mark is already in use, evidence of use from the earliest date is worth collecting now — invoices, packaging, advertisements. It is the single most useful thing to have if an objection comes.

Order of operations

Naming a new company?

Check the trademark register before you reserve the company name, not after. A name approved by the company registry can still infringe a registered mark, and the rebrand is far more expensive than the search was.

Before you call

Questions we are asked about this

Can I use the ™ symbol before registration?

The ™ symbol can be used once an application is filed. The ® symbol may be used only after the mark is registered, and using it earlier is itself an offence.

How long does registration take?

The application number is issued immediately, and protection is claimed from the date of filing. Registration itself depends on whether an examination report or an opposition arises, and can run from several months to considerably longer.

Someone is already using my name. Is there anything to do?

It depends on who used it first, whether either party has registration, and in which class and territory. Prior use carries weight in Indian law even without registration, so the facts and the dates decide this rather than the register alone.

Do I need to register in every class?

No. Register where you trade and where you realistically will. Over-filing is expensive to maintain and vulnerable to removal for non-use.
