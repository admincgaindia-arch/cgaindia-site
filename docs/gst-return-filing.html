GST return filing

A fixed monthly cycle, with credit reconciled against GSTR-2B before the return goes in rather than after a notice asks why it did not match.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

Input tax credit is now a function of what your supplier filed, not of what you paid. If a vendor misses GSTR-1, the credit does not appear in your 2B and claiming it anyway creates an exposure that surfaces months later as a mismatch notice.

So reconciliation comes before filing, every month, and the vendors who are costing you credit are named while there is still time to chase them.

What we handle

  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B on the monthly cycle, and the QRMP quarterly cycle with monthly payment
  • GSTR-2B reconciliation against purchase records, with a vendor-wise list of missing credit
  • Input tax credit eligibility review, including blocked credit under section 17(5)
  • Reversal working under rules 42 and 43 for exempt and personal use
  • Output liability review — rate, place of supply, and reverse charge on notified supplies
  • Annual return in GSTR-9 and reconciliation statement in GSTR-9C where applicable
  • E-invoice and e-way bill compliance checks where turnover crosses the threshold
  • Amendment of earlier returns, and credit note treatment within the allowed window
  • Reply to scrutiny notices in ASMT-10, and to demands in DRC-01 and DRC-01A
  • Refund applications — export without payment of tax, inverted duty structure and excess balance

What you supply

  • Sales register for the period, with invoice-wise details
  • Purchase register, with supplier GSTIN and invoice numbers
  • Bank statements for the period
  • Debit and credit notes issued and received
  • Details of any advance received, and of exempt or non-GST supply
  • Portal credentials, or access as an authorised representative

If your accounting software exports the sales and purchase registers directly, send those files. Re-keying data is where errors enter.

Questions we are asked about this

Can I claim credit that is not showing in my GSTR-2B?

The law ties credit to what appears in your auto-populated statement, subject to the other conditions being met. The practical answer is to get the supplier to report the invoice rather than to claim and defend it later.

What if a return for an earlier month was never filed?

Returns are sequential — a gap blocks the ones after it, late fee and interest accumulate, and prolonged non-filing can lead to suspension of the registration. It is usually cheaper to clear the backlog than to carry it.

Do we need GSTR-9C?

The annual return and the reconciliation statement have separate turnover thresholds, and they change from time to time. We confirm your position for the year before preparing either.

We received an ASMT-10 asking about a difference between 3B and 2A.

That is a scrutiny notice with a reply window. Most of these are explained by timing differences or credit notes. Send it across with the return data and we will draft the reply.

Send us three months of returns

We will tell you what credit you are losing, where the liability is understated, and what a clean monthly cycle would cost.
