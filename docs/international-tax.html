International taxation

Payments leaving India carry two separate questions — how much tax to withhold, and which reporting the transaction triggers. Both are settled before the remittance, not after.

Get a quote Ask on WhatsApp

A foreign remittance can be a royalty, fees for technical services, business income or a capital account transaction, and each attracts a different treatment. The bank will not release the funds without a certificate, and a certificate issued on the wrong characterisation creates a liability on the payer, not the recipient.

Where the parties are related, transfer pricing applies in addition. The requirement is not merely to price the transaction fairly but to be able to show, in prescribed form and at the right time, how the price was arrived at.

What we handle

  • Withholding under section 195 on payments to non-residents, with treaty rate applied where available
  • Form 15CA and Form 15CB certification for remittances
  • Characterisation of payments — royalty, fees for technical services, business profits, interest, dividend
  • Permanent establishment and business connection exposure review
  • Equalisation levy and digital transaction implications where relevant
  • Transfer pricing study, benchmarking and documentation, with Form 3CEB
  • Inbound structuring for a foreign parent setting up an Indian subsidiary or liaison office
  • FDI reporting on the RBI FIRMS portal, including Form FC-GPR and FC-TRS
  • Overseas direct investment reporting and the annual performance return
  • Application for a lower or nil withholding certificate under section 195(2)

What you supply

  • The agreement or invoice underlying the remittance
  • Tax residency certificate of the recipient, and Form 10F
  • No permanent establishment declaration from the recipient, where applicable
  • Details of the relationship between the parties, if any
  • Board resolution and shareholding pattern, for capital account transactions
  • Bank details and the purpose code proposed by the bank

Send the agreement before the invoice is raised where possible. The way a contract is drafted often decides the withholding rate more than the payment itself does.

Questions we are asked about this

The recipient says their country has a treaty, so no tax should be deducted.

A treaty rate is available only where the recipient furnishes a tax residency certificate and Form 10F, and where the payment falls within the article relied on. Without those documents the domestic rate applies, and the liability for short deduction sits with the payer in India.

Is Form 15CA needed for every foreign payment?

No. Certain payments are specified as not requiring it, and for some categories only part of the form is required. Which applies depends on the nature and amount of the remittance, which is exactly what we determine first.

We are a small company with one foreign related party. Does transfer pricing apply?

If the transaction is with an associated enterprise, the requirement applies regardless of company size, and the accountant's report in Form 3CEB is due on its own timeline. The volume of documentation varies with the value of the transactions.

A foreign company wants to set up in India. Where do we start?

With the structure — subsidiary, branch, liaison office or project office each carry different tax and FEMA consequences. That choice is easier to make at the start than to reverse afterwards.
