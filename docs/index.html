Before you call

Questions we are asked every week

How quickly can a company be registered?

Once name approval and signed documents are in hand, incorporation is usually a matter of days rather than weeks. The variable is document readiness — identity proofs, address proof for the registered office and the no objection certificate from the property owner. We give you a realistic date at the scope stage, not an optimistic one.

What will it cost?

Two components: our professional fee, and the statutory or government fee, which is fixed by the authority and varies with state, authorised capital, turnover or category. We quote both separately in writing before starting. Anyone quoting a single all-India number without asking about your state is guessing.

Do I need to visit an office?

No. Most matters run entirely online — documents collected digitally, signatures through a digital signature certificate or e-sign. If you would rather sit across a desk, any of our five offices works.

Can you work with clients based outside India?

Yes. Non-resident founders, NRI taxpayers and foreign parent companies are a regular part of the practice. Everything is delivered from India, and we handle apostille and notarisation requirements where the authority asks for them.

I already have an accountant. Can you handle only part of the work?

Yes. Many clients keep their existing bookkeeping and bring us in for licensing, trademark, ROC or a specific notice. We will not push a bundle you do not need.

I received a notice. What now?

Send it across before the reply window closes — GST, income tax, ROC and pollution board notices all carry deadlines, and an extension is easier to obtain before the date than after. We read it, tell you how serious it is, and quote for the reply.
